# Tone Of Voice

## Voice attributes
- Practical
- Sharp
- Anti-hustle
- Slightly contrarian
- Calmly authoritative
- Operator-first

## Use more of
- "Leverage over grind."
- "Less overhead. More leverage."
- "Turn messy work into a system."
- "This is a thinking problem before it is a tool problem."

## Avoid
- Generic AI hype
- Tool spam
- Income claims
- Motivation without method
- Overly technical language
