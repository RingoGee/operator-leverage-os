# OLS Product Bridge Reviewer

## Purpose
Evaluate whether a piece of content, content cluster, or audience signal should connect to Proof Stack or another paid/free implementation asset.

## When to use
Use after strong content performance, repeated audience questions, product-interest clicks, or when deciding whether to create a lead magnet, waitlist, product, template, or implementation asset.

## Inputs required
- Content topic or carousel
- Performance data or audience signal
- Optional product idea
- Optional business objective

## Source files to read
- `05-products/product-ladder.md`
- `05-products/paid-product-ideas.md`
- `05-products/razorpay-product-catalog.csv`
- `05-products/subscription-offer-notes.md`
- `02-editorial-system/free-vs-paid-logic.md`
- `06-analytics/post-performance-tracker.csv`
- `06-analytics/payment-performance-tracker.csv`
- Relevant carousel files

## Output files to create or update
If explicitly requested:

- `05-products/product-ladder.md`
- `05-products/paid-product-ideas.md`
- `05-products/razorpay-product-catalog.csv`
- `05-products/subscription-offer-notes.md`
- `07-exports/product-bridge-notes/`

Review should evaluate:

- Is the free content intellectually complete?
- Is there repeated implementation demand?
- Does the audience need a template/system/tracker/SOP?
- Is this a lead magnet, low-ticket product, subscription asset, or premium product?
- Is Proof Stack relevant?
- Should the bridge be absent, soft, or direct?
- What is the right product hypothesis?
- What should be validated before building?

## Step-by-step procedure
1. Read free vs paid logic and product files.
2. Analyze content/product fit.
3. Identify demand signals.
4. Decide whether to bridge to:
   - no asset
   - free lead magnet
   - waitlist
   - Proof Stack
   - another paid product
   - future subscription
5. Recommend next action.
6. Update product files only if explicitly asked.

## Guardrails
- Do not create payment links.
- Do not create checkout flows.
- Do not create Razorpay integration.
- Do not create product pages unless asked.
- Do not weaken free content.
- Do not force paid conversion.
- Do not treat one good post as validation.
- Do not create actual products unless explicitly requested.

## Done when
- Product bridge recommendation is clear.
- Required validation is stated.
- Next step is defined.

## Summary format
1. Content/product reviewed
2. Product bridge verdict
3. Demand signals
4. Recommended asset type
5. Suggested bridge language
6. Validation needed
7. Files changed, if any
