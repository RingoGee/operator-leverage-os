# Hook Bank

## Strategy Systems
- You do not need more dashboards. You need a better decision layer.
- Most business problems are not execution problems. They are framing problems.

## Founder Leverage
- Founders do not need more productivity hacks. They need fewer recurring decisions.
- If your business depends on memory, you do not have a system yet.

## Capital & Narrative
- Investors do not just buy traction. They buy proof that you understand the machine.
- A data room is not storage. It is a trust-building system.

## Corporate Leverage
- Appraisal season is not won in the final review. It is won in the evidence bank.
- Visibility is not bragging. It is structured proof of useful work.
