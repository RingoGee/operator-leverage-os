# Visual Execution Pack: Capital Concentration Proof Stack

## Final design direction
Build an 8-slide Operator Leverage OS carousel as a visual strategy memo. The deck should explain that fundraising communication must prove allocation readiness in a concentrated capital market.

## Format decision
- Format: Instagram carousel, 8 slides.
- Style: infographic-led, boardroom-ready, operating-system diagram.
- Central visual object: capital allocation funnel → investor doubt conveyor → five-layer proof stack.

## Locked slide chrome
- Top-left: `Capital & Narrative`
- Top-right: slide number, e.g., `01 / 08`
- Bottom-left: `Operator Leverage OS`
- Bottom-right: source/caveat marker where needed
- Keep chrome small and consistent.

## Visual identity direction
- Warm ivory background.
- Charcoal typography and diagram lines.
- Deep navy or forest green proof accents.
- Electric blue only for the attention bottleneck.
- Muted red only for investor doubts.

## Slide-by-slide visual production instructions

### Slide 1
Create a wide funnel labeled “capital available” that narrows into a tiny gate labeled “attention.” Put the headline above: “Capital is available. Attention is not.” Add subline: “Are you allocatable?”

### Slide 2
Create a three-gate filtering diagram: plausible → diligence-worthy → allocation-ready. Use many small dots before the first gate and only a few after the final gate.

### Slide 3
Create three evidence cards with small source tags:
1. Carta: `$119.5B raised in 2025 on Carta`
2. Carta: `4,859 rounds — lowest in at least six years`
3. NVCA: `AI captured 65%+ of 2025 venture investment`
Add caveat strip: “Market signal, not a company-level guarantee.”

### Slide 4
Create a split screen. Left card: “Louder deck” with messy labels: bigger TAM, more logos, more AI language. Right card: “Proof stack” with clean labels: timing, urgency, traction quality, defensibility, capital logic.

### Slide 5
Create a conveyor belt. Investor doubt cards enter on the left: why now, urgency, growth quality, moat, use of funds, next proof. Checked proof blocks exit on the right.

### Slide 6
Create the five-layer Allocation Proof Stack as the hero visual:
1. Market pull — why now?
2. Problem urgency — why must buyers act?
3. Traction quality — why is growth real?
4. Defensibility — why can this compound?
5. Capital logic — why does this round unlock the next proof point?

### Slide 7
Create a before/after narrative rewrite. Left: “We are an AI workflow platform for finance teams.” Right: “Finance teams are moving from manual reconciliation to exception-based control...” Add tags: timing, urgency, usage depth, capital logic. Include tiny note: “Fictional example for structure.”

### Slide 8
Create a clean checklist card with the question: “Which investor doubt does this remove?” Add small proof-stack checklist beneath. End with soft profile line.

## Master prompt draft
Create an 8-slide Instagram carousel in the Operator Leverage OS style: warm ivory background, charcoal diagram lines, deep navy/forest proof accents, electric blue bottleneck highlight, muted red doubt markers. The carousel must look like a premium visual strategy memo, not a social media listicle. Use a consistent locked chrome: top-left section label, top-right slide number, bottom-left Operator Leverage OS. Build the story around one central visual system: capital allocation funnel, investor attention gates, doubt-removal conveyor, and five-layer proof stack. Keep text minimal but high-signal. Make slide 6 screenshot-worthy. Include source tags and caveat on slide 3. Do not use stock startup imagery, rocket ships, money bags, AI icons, or hype aesthetics.

## Individual slide prompts
1. Funnel tension visual showing capital narrowing into attention gate.
2. Three-gate investor allocation filter.
3. Three-card evidence stack with Carta and NVCA source tags.
4. Split-screen louder deck vs proof stack.
5. Doubt-removal conveyor.
6. Five-layer Allocation Proof Stack.
7. Before/after fundraising narrative rewrite.
8. Operator checklist with final behavior question.

## Quality guardrails
- Do not invent new numbers.
- Do not add extra claims beyond the source-backed evidence cards.
- Do not make the carousel tool-led.
- Do not make slide 6 cluttered.
- Do not generate images inside Codex.
