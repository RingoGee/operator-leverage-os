# Design Prompts: Liquidity Pressure Narrative

## Overall visual direction
Create an 8-slide visual strategy memo about fundraising communication under liquidity pressure. The central visual object is a pressure cascade that becomes a five-rung liquidity-aware narrative ladder.

## Premium analytical identity
- Calm investment memo, not market panic.
- Sharp, structured, and useful for founders/operators preparing investor materials.
- Visuals should explain the path from LP pressure to founder narrative discipline.

## Locked slide chrome system
- Top-left: “Capital & Narrative”.
- Top-right: slide number.
- Bottom-left: `Operator Leverage OS`.
- Bottom-right: source/caveat note on evidence slide.

## Visual identity direction
- Warm off-white background.
- Charcoal primary text and diagram lines.
- Deep green or blue accent for credible value path.
- Muted red for pressure, not alarm.
- Use grid-paper or memo-panel feel.

## Signature accent palette
- Background: warm ivory.
- Primary: charcoal.
- Value path: deep green or navy.
- Pressure signal: muted red.
- Evidence card background: pale sand.

## Typography direction
- Strong headline, clear labels.
- Small source tags.
- Minimal sentence copy.
- Framework labels should be readable at mobile size.

## Mobile readability rules
- One primary diagram per slide.
- Avoid finance jargon density.
- Each slide should have one clean takeaway.
- Evidence numbers must be legible.

## Slide-by-slide visual directions

### Slide 1
- **Composition archetype:** Tension chart.
- **Dominant element:** Growth line compressed by liquidity pressure block.
- **Headline mode:** Contrarian claim.
- **Visual direction:** Show a rising growth line pressed down or constrained by a labeled pressure block.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Investors are not just buying growth. They are managing liquidity pressure.”

### Slide 2
- **Composition archetype:** Cascade map.
- **Dominant element:** LP → fund → founder cascade.
- **Headline mode:** Structural diagnosis.
- **Visual direction:** Three stacked layers with downward pressure arrows and upward proof arrows.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Your investor has investors too.”

### Slide 3
- **Composition archetype:** Evidence stack.
- **Dominant element:** Three evidence cards.
- **Headline mode:** Evidence label.
- **Visual direction:** Show “improved but uneven” with cards for exits, unicorn backlog, and top-deal concentration.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Exits improved. Liquidity pressure remains.”

### Slide 4
- **Composition archetype:** Split-screen contrast.
- **Dominant element:** Upside story vs value-realization path.
- **Headline mode:** Antithesis.
- **Visual direction:** Left side shows a large but fuzzy future bubble. Right side shows a clear path of milestones and buyers.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Bigger ambition ≠ clearer value path.”

### Slide 5
- **Composition archetype:** Mechanism chain.
- **Dominant element:** Next-buyer chain.
- **Headline mode:** Mechanism reveal.
- **Visual direction:** Link current investor, next investor, strategic buyer, and LP with “what must each believe?” labels.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Investors listen for the next buyer's belief.”

### Slide 6
- **Composition archetype:** Ladder framework.
- **Dominant element:** Five-rung Liquidity-Aware Narrative Ladder.
- **Headline mode:** Named model.
- **Visual direction:** Five clean rungs moving from growth to realization.
- **Minimal slide text:** Growth / Quality / Efficiency / Optionality / Realization.

### Slide 7
- **Composition archetype:** Before/after rewrite.
- **Dominant element:** Investor update rewrite card.
- **Headline mode:** Applied example.
- **Visual direction:** Show the sentence “We grew enterprise pipeline” becoming a more legible value-path statement with ladder tags.
- **Minimal slide text:** “Add the value-realization line.”

### Slide 8
- **Composition archetype:** Checklist prompt.
- **Dominant element:** One-line operating prompt.
- **Headline mode:** Behavior shift.
- **Visual direction:** Prompt card over a faint ladder watermark.
- **Minimal slide text:** “What does this progress make more believable next?”

## Evidence slide direction
Use source labels for NVCA and PitchBook-NVCA. Keep caveat visible: “Market context, not a company-level prediction.”

## Antithesis slide direction
Do not make the growth side wrong. Show that growth is necessary but incomplete without path quality.

## Framework slide direction
Slide 6 should feel like a reusable investor narrative worksheet.

## Application slide direction
Use “fictional example for structure” as a small note.

## Final profile-entry slide direction
Use a soft category promise: “Visual systems for strategy, fundraising, and executive communication.”

## What to avoid
- Market panic aesthetics.
- IPO hype.
- Overly technical LP/fund diagrams.
- Guarantees about exits, markups, or acquisitions.
- Generic finance stock icons.

## Design QA checklist
- [ ] The pressure cascade is easy to understand.
- [ ] The ladder is the save-worthy center of the carousel.
- [ ] Evidence is source-labeled and caveated.
- [ ] Growth is framed as necessary but incomplete.
- [ ] No images or designed slides are generated in this file.
