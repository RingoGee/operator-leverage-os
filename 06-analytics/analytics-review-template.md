## F. Benchmarks
These are working targets, not guarantees. Compare against the channel’s own rolling 10-post median over time.

- **Swipe-through / completion:** 50%+ target for strong carousels.
- **Save rate:** 2%+ strong; 5–10% excellent for high-value carousels.
- **Profile visit rate:** 1.5–3% strong.
- **Bio click rate:** early working target is 8–15% of profile visits.
- **Context rule:** engagement benchmarks vary heavily by account size, niche, and format, so internal trend matters more than generic benchmarks.

## J. Product-interest signals
Review whether the audience is showing demand for implementation assets such as Proof Stack or another operating kit.

### Demand signals to watch
- Saves on visibility, evidence, work-to-impact, appraisal, stakeholder update, and upward communication posts.
- Shares on antithesis posts that explain common workplace pain.
- Profile visits from decision tools, operating system breakdowns, and evidence-backed case lenses.
- Bio clicks from relevant lead-magnet, waitlist, or product bridges.
- Comments or replies mentioning visibility, proof, appraisal, manager updates, client updates, or stakeholder communication.
- Purchase intent clicks from posts connected to paid implementation assets.
- Inquiries asking for templates, trackers, examples, or implementation help.
- Repeated qualitative language that suggests the same pain is showing up across segments.
