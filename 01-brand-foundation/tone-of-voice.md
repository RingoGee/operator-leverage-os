# Tone Of Voice

## Purpose of this file
Use this file to keep the brand's writing sharp, useful, calm, and operator-grade across posts, captions, product pages, and repo notes.

## How the brand speaks
- Practical: every idea should help someone think, decide, communicate, or operate better.
- Sharp: remove soft filler, vague motivation, and inflated claims.
- Calm: no fake urgency, panic, or trend-chasing.
- Slightly contrarian: challenge lazy assumptions without sounding cynical.
- Visual: write in a way that can become a diagram, map, scorecard, or control panel.
- Operator-grade: sound like someone who understands messy work, not someone selling hacks.

## Use more of
- "This is a thinking problem before it is a tool problem."
- "Turn messy work into a system."
- "Visibility is structured proof, not bragging."
- "AI should compress execution, not replace judgment."
- "Less overhead. More leverage."

## Use less of
- "You need these 10 AI tools."
- "This prompt will change your life."
- "Escape the 9-5 with AI."
- "Crush your goals."
- "Everyone is doing this. Don't get left behind."

## Do / don't examples

### Do
- "Most MIS reports fail because they collect data without defining the decision layer."
- "Before using AI, decide what the output needs to clarify."
- "A good appraisal file is not a brag sheet. It is an evidence system."

### Don't
- "Here are 15 AI tools you must try today."
- "Use this secret prompt to 10x your income."
- "Hustle harder until the algorithm rewards you."

## Language rules
- Prefer clear nouns: system, decision, evidence, leverage, signal, rhythm, proof, context.
- Avoid hype words: insane, secret, guaranteed, viral, passive income, ultimate hack.
- Avoid guru language that talks down to the reader.
- Do not make tools the hero. Make the operating model the hero.
