# Visual Execution Pack: The Investor Update Is Becoming a Diligence File

## Final design direction
Build an 8-slide Operator Leverage OS carousel showing that investor updates should operate as structured diligence records, not casual monthly emails.

## Format decision
- Format: Instagram carousel, 8 slides.
- Style: document-system visual strategy memo.
- Central visual object: update email → diligence folder → continuity timeline → six-part update control panel.

## Locked slide chrome
- Top-left: `Capital & Narrative`
- Top-right: slide number, e.g., `01 / 08`
- Bottom-left: `Operator Leverage OS`
- Bottom-right: source/caveat marker where relevant

## Visual identity direction
- Warm ivory background.
- Charcoal text and linework.
- Deep blue or green for trust/continuity.
- Muted red for broken KPI drift.
- Modular cards, timeline spine, and folder/document metaphors.

## Slide-by-slide visual production instructions

### Slide 1
Show an investor update email card folding or transforming into a structured diligence folder. Headline: “Your investor update is not an email.” Subline: “It is diligence before diligence.”

### Slide 2
Show a broken timeline with three monthly cards. Each card uses different metric labels or missing context. Add headline: “Random updates create trust gaps.”

### Slide 3
Show two evidence cards connected to a data-room icon:
1. PitchBook/Web Summit: investors use AI to summarize diligence materials and identify deals.
2. Findex: precision, structure, and consistent health metrics matter.
Add caveat: “AI-assisted does not mean fully automated.”

### Slide 4
Create split screen: “Pretty update” vs “Trusted record.” The pretty side has polish but unstable metrics. The trusted side has stable metrics, context, risks, asks, next proof.

### Slide 5
Create a continuity spine linking six monthly update cards. Each card has the same fields: baseline, movement, meaning, risk, ask, next proof. Headline: “Trust compounds through continuity.”

### Slide 6
Create a 2x3 control panel titled “The Diligence Trail Update.” Modules: Baseline, Movement, Meaning, Risk, Ask, Next proof.

### Slide 7
Create before/after update rewrite. Left: “Revenue was up and we hired two people.” Right: expansion revenue, burn increase, next proof about pipeline coverage. Add tags: movement, meaning, risk, next proof. Include “Fictional example for structure.”

### Slide 8
Show six update cards leading to a trust signal. Headline: “Write for the six-update reader.” Add final question: “Would six updates in sequence make the business easier to trust?”

## Master prompt draft
Create an 8-slide Instagram carousel in the Operator Leverage OS style: warm document background, charcoal typography, deep blue/green trust accents, muted red warnings for broken timelines. The carousel should feel like a founder's office investor communication system. Use consistent locked chrome across slides. Build around one visual transformation: email becomes diligence folder, then continuity timeline, then six-part update control panel. Keep copy minimal, structured, and mobile-readable. Make slide 6 screenshot-worthy as a reusable framework. Include source tags and caveat on slide 3. Do not create a software dashboard, do not use generic SaaS UI screenshots, and do not claim updates guarantee fundraising.

## Individual slide prompts
1. Email-to-diligence-folder transformation.
2. Broken KPI timeline.
3. Evidence cards with source tags and caveat.
4. Pretty update vs trusted record split-screen.
5. Six-month continuity spine.
6. Six-module Diligence Trail Update control panel.
7. Before/after update rewrite.
8. Six-update reader behavior checklist.

## Quality guardrails
- Do not imply AI replaces investor judgment.
- Do not create a dashboard product visual.
- Do not add fake follow-on funding statistics.
- Keep the framework stable and legible.
- Do not generate images inside Codex.
