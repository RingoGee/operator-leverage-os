# Subscription Offer Notes

## Purpose of this file
Use this file to think about a future recurring offer. Subscription is a later layer, not the first monetization move.

## Subscription principle
Do not launch a subscription until free content and one-off products show demand. Start with Proof Stack, learn from buyer behavior, then consider recurring support.

## When subscription may make sense
A subscription may make sense after:
- Free content has consistent saves, shares, and replies.
- At least one low-ticket product has paying customers.
- Buyers ask for more examples, reviews, or ongoing implementation help.
- The brand has enough proven workflows to support monthly drops without lowering quality.

## Possible future promise
Monthly implementation support for operators who want to turn messy work into clearer systems, communication, proof, and decisions.

## Possible inclusions
- Monthly implementation drops
- Workflow/system teardowns
- Editable canvases
- Subscriber-only diagnostics
- Office-hours style Q&A notes
- Example-filled asset walkthroughs
- Prompt systems attached to specific workflows
- Review checklists

## What the subscription should not be
- Not a generic prompt library.
- Not an AI tools feed.
- Not an IG Subscription dependency.
- Not a content dump with no implementation path.
- Not a promise of income, promotion, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Possible monthly themes
- Visibility and evidence
- Decision-ready communication
- Founder operating rhythm
- Strategy decision layers
- Narrative and proof systems

## Launch guardrail
Only consider subscription after the audience has shown demand for one-off implementation assets. Recurring revenue should come after proven utility, not before it.
