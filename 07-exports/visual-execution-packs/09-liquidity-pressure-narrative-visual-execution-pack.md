# Visual Execution Pack: Liquidity Pressure Narrative

## Final design direction
Build an 8-slide Operator Leverage OS carousel showing that investor communication should connect growth to value-realization logic when liquidity pressure shapes investor behavior.

## Format decision
- Format: Instagram carousel, 8 slides.
- Style: calm investment-memo diagram.
- Central visual object: liquidity pressure cascade → next-buyer chain → five-rung narrative ladder.

## Locked slide chrome
- Top-left: `Capital & Narrative`
- Top-right: slide number, e.g., `01 / 08`
- Bottom-left: `Operator Leverage OS`
- Bottom-right: source/caveat marker on evidence slide

## Visual identity direction
- Warm ivory / memo-paper background.
- Charcoal typography and grid lines.
- Deep green or navy for credible value path.
- Muted red for liquidity pressure.
- No panic visuals.

## Slide-by-slide visual production instructions

### Slide 1
Show a clean growth chart compressed by a block labeled “liquidity pressure.” Headline: “Investors are not just buying growth.” Support: “They are managing liquidity pressure.”

### Slide 2
Show a three-layer cascade: LPs want distributions → funds need credible return paths → founders must make value legible. Use downward pressure arrows and upward proof arrows.

### Slide 3
Create three evidence cards:
1. NVCA: 2025 exits more than doubled YoY but remain insufficient for backlog.
2. NVCA: 859 unicorns valued at $4.34T; 30–40 unicorn exits last year.
3. PitchBook-NVCA: Q1 2026 records were highly concentrated in the five largest deals/exits.
Add caveat: “Market context, not a company-level prediction.”

### Slide 4
Create split screen: left is “bigger future” with fuzzy TAM bubble; right is “clear value path” with milestones, quality, efficiency, optionality, realization.

### Slide 5
Show a next-buyer belief chain: current investor → next investor → strategic buyer → LP. Each card asks, “What must they believe?”

### Slide 6
Create the five-rung Liquidity-Aware Narrative Ladder: Growth, Quality, Efficiency, Optionality, Realization. Add one question per rung.

### Slide 7
Create a before/after investor update sentence. Left: “We grew enterprise pipeline.” Right: “Enterprise pipeline grew in the segment most likely to support expansion revenue, strategic partnerships, and future acquisition logic.” Add tags: quality, optionality, realization. Mark as fictional example.

### Slide 8
Show a large prompt card over faint ladder watermark: “What does this progress make more believable next?” Add profile reason line.

## Master prompt draft
Create an 8-slide Instagram carousel in the Operator Leverage OS style: warm memo-paper background, charcoal diagrams, deep green/navy value-path accents, muted red pressure markers. The visual tone must be calm and analytical, not panic-driven. Use consistent locked chrome. Build the story around one visual system: liquidity pressure cascade, next-buyer belief chain, and five-rung narrative ladder. Keep text minimal and high-signal. Make slide 6 screenshot-worthy. Include source tags and caveat on slide 3. Do not use generic finance stock imagery, IPO hype, rocket ships, or guaranteed exit language.

## Individual slide prompts
1. Growth chart under liquidity pressure.
2. LP-to-fund-to-founder pressure cascade.
3. Evidence card stack with NVCA and PitchBook-NVCA source tags.
4. Upside story vs value-realization path.
5. Next-buyer belief chain.
6. Five-rung Liquidity-Aware Narrative Ladder.
7. Before/after update rewrite with ladder tags.
8. Operating prompt and profile reason.

## Quality guardrails
- Do not say exits are easy or fully back.
- Do not guarantee markups, acquisitions, or IPO outcomes.
- Do not turn the carousel into finance jargon.
- Keep the pressure visual calm.
- Do not generate images inside Codex.
