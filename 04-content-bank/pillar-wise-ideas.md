# Pillar-Wise Ideas

## Purpose of this file
Use this file to organize idea territories by pillar. These are directions for future carousel briefs, not finished posts.

## 1. Strategy Systems

### Strategic role of the pillar
Strategy Systems teaches operators how to turn ambiguous business problems into decision-ready structures.

### Strong idea territories
- Decision layers for messy business problems
- Problem decomposition before solution mode
- MIS-to-decision translation
- Tradeoff maps for executive recommendations
- Signal versus noise in growth work
- Client problem framing
- GTM assumption stacks
- Meeting-to-decision conversion
- Decision rights for cross-functional work
- Executive context compression

### Best visual object types
- Decision tree
- Decision layer
- Operating map
- Scorecard
- Funnel
- Teardown map
- Work-to-impact chain

### Paid asset directions
- Decision memo templates
- Business problem canvases
- GTM decomposition sheets
- MIS decision-layer templates
- Strategy review checklists
- Client diagnostic canvases

### Topics to avoid
- Generic strategy quotes
- Tool-led market research tutorials
- Trend commentary without a decision principle
- Business theory with no operator utility
- AI tool lists for strategy work
