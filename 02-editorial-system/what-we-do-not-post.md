# What We Do Not Post

## Avoid
- Generic AI tool lists
- Prompt dumps
- "Make money with AI" content
- Motivation-only posts
- Text-heavy carousels
- Trend-chasing without strategic fit
- Tool tutorials without a mental model
- Overpromising income, speed, or outcomes

## Editorial filter
If the post attracts people who only want shortcuts, rewrite it for people who want leverage.
