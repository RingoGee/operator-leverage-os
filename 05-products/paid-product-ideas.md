# Paid Product Ideas

## Purpose of this file
Use this file to prioritize paid product directions. These are product directions only, not finished products.

## Product rule
Every paid product must help the buyer implement a mental model faster. Do not sell generic prompt dumps, tool lists, or vague advice.

## First product

### Proof Stack
**Audience:** Corporate professionals globally, strategy and growth operators, founder's office and chief-of-staff professionals, consultants, and small advisory teams.

**Problem solved:** The buyer is doing valuable work, but it is not converted into visible evidence, sharper updates, or career/business leverage for managers, founders, clients, or stakeholders.

**Free content bridge:** Work-to-impact chain, visibility without bragging, appraisal evidence bank, stakeholder update map, MIS decision layer, upward communication ladder, and decision-ready email model.

**Deliverables:**
- Evidence Bank
- Work-to-Impact Chain
- Weekly Signal Panel
- Short implementation guide
- Optional prompt system attached to the update workflow

**Why someone would pay:** They understand the free mental model but want a ready-to-use system to capture evidence, turn work into proof, and communicate operating value faster.

**Risk/complexity:** Medium. The product must stay globally relevant while still feeling concrete enough for corporate and operator workflows.

## Near-term products

### Decision Memo Kit
**Audience:** Strategy and growth operators, founder's office professionals, consultants, and advisory teams.

**Problem solved:** Recommendations are shared without enough context, options, tradeoffs, or decision clarity.

**Free content bridge:** Decision layer for messy business problems, tradeoff map for executive recommendations, meeting-to-decision conversion, and executive context compression.

**Deliverables:**
- Decision memo template
- Tradeoff scorecard
- Executive context compression guide
- Meeting-to-decision note structure
- Example-filled recommendation formats

**Why someone would pay:** They need to look more senior in decision rooms and reduce back-and-forth around recommendations.

**Risk/complexity:** Low. The scope is focused and easy to connect to multiple free posts.

### Appraisal Evidence Tracker
**Audience:** Corporate professionals who need to show proof of impact during reviews, promotions, or internal transitions.

**Problem solved:** Useful work is forgotten, scattered, or poorly framed when performance conversations happen.

**Free content bridge:** Appraisal evidence bank, work-to-impact chain, visibility without bragging, and upward communication ladder.

**Deliverables:**
- Evidence capture tracker
- Impact framing worksheet
- Manager update template
- Review preparation checklist
- Example evidence entries

**Why someone would pay:** They want to turn ongoing work into credible proof before high-stakes review conversations.

**Risk/complexity:** Low to medium. Timing may matter around appraisal cycles, but the underlying use case is evergreen.

### Executive Update Template Pack
**Audience:** Founder’s office professionals, operators, consultants, managers, and advisory teams.

**Problem solved:** Updates become long activity lists instead of decision-ready communication.

**Free content bridge:** Executive context compression, decision-ready email model, stakeholder map, and board update decision layer.

**Deliverables:**
- Executive update template
- Stakeholder-specific update formats
- Decision-ready email structure
- Risk/ask/action checklist
- Example-filled update transformations

**Why someone would pay:** They need reusable communication formats that make their thinking clearer and more senior.

**Risk/complexity:** Low. Strong bridge from free communication content.

### Founder Control Panel
**Audience:** Solopreneurs, early-stage founders, founder's office professionals, and small advisory teams.

**Problem solved:** Founders track too many things and still lack a clear operating view.

**Free content bridge:** Founder control panel for lean teams, recurring decision audit, bottleneck map, and weekly operating rhythm.

**Deliverables:**
- Founder control panel template
- Weekly review rhythm
- Bottleneck diagnosis canvas
- Recurring decision tracker
- Implementation guide

**Why someone would pay:** They want a simple operating panel to reduce mental overhead and improve weekly decision quality.

**Risk/complexity:** Medium. Needs careful scoping to avoid becoming too broad.

## Later products

### Fundraise Proof Stack System
**Audience:** Early-stage founders and founder's office professionals preparing for investor conversations.

**Problem solved:** Founders have traction and progress but lack a structured proof system that builds investor confidence.

**Free content bridge:** Investor proof stack, narrative spine, data room as trust system, traction-to-repeatability map, and investor FAQ evidence map.

**Deliverables:**
- Proof stack tracker
- Investor update template
- FAQ evidence bank
- Data room planning canvas
- Risk narrative worksheet

**Why someone would pay:** They need to organize evidence and narrative before high-stakes fundraising conversations.

**Risk/complexity:** High. Requires more examples, nuance, and trust before selling.

### Proposal Factory Kit
**Audience:** Solopreneurs, consultants, and small advisory teams.

**Problem solved:** Proposals take too long, vary in quality, and fail to communicate diagnosis, proof, and next steps clearly.

**Free content bridge:** Proposal factory without generic templates, offer clarity map, proof hierarchy, and case study proof stack.

**Deliverables:**
- Proposal structure
- Diagnostic intake canvas
- Proof section builder
- Scope and risk checklist
- Example proposal sections

**Why someone would pay:** They want to package judgment and sell advisory work with more clarity and less rebuilding.

**Risk/complexity:** Medium. Strong revenue relevance, but may pull the brand toward consultants if launched too early.

### Strategy Decision System
**Audience:** Strategy operators, growth leads, consultants, and founder's office professionals.

**Problem solved:** Teams have data, discussions, and opinions but lack a repeatable decision system.

**Free content bridge:** Decision layer, problem decomposition, GTM assumption stack, signal versus noise, and decision rights map.

**Deliverables:**
- Decision system guide
- Problem framing canvas
- Assumption tracker
- Decision rights map
- Review checklist

**Why someone would pay:** They need a reusable operating system for making clearer decisions across projects.

**Risk/complexity:** High. It should come after the audience already trusts the brand's strategy content.

## Product sequencing note
Start with Proof Stack. Use demand signals from saves, replies, comments, and lead magnet usage before building larger systems.
