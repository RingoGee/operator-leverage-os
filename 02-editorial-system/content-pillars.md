# Content Pillars

## Purpose of this file
Use this file to decide where an editorial idea belongs. Pillars should stay mental-model-led and should not be defined by tools.

## Pillar rule
Each pillar must help the audience turn messy work into clearer thinking, communication, decisions, or systems.

## 1. Strategy Systems

### What this pillar teaches
How to decompose business problems, evaluate tradeoffs, design decision layers, and turn unclear strategy work into structured operating logic.

### Audience pressure it addresses
Operators and teams are expected to make decisions from messy inputs, incomplete context, unclear ownership, and conflicting priorities.

### Mental models that belong here
- Problem decomposition
- Decision layers
- Signal vs noise
- GTM logic
- Tradeoff mapping
- Assumption testing
- MIS-to-decision translation

### Visual objects that work best
- Decision trees
- Operating maps
- Scorecards
- Funnels
- Teardown maps
- Before/after systems

### Paid assets this can lead to
- Decision memo templates
- Business problem canvases
- GTM decomposition sheets
- MIS decision-layer templates
- Strategy review checklists

### Exclude from this pillar
- Generic strategy quotes
- Tool-led market research workflows
- Abstract business theory with no operator utility
- Trend commentary that does not improve decision quality

## 2. Founder Leverage

### What this pillar teaches
How founders, solopreneurs, and founder's office or chief-of-staff professionals can reduce recurring decisions, install operating rhythms, and create leverage without adding overhead.

### Audience pressure it addresses
Founders and lean teams must sell, deliver, manage priorities, communicate, and build systems while moving faster than their available time or team size allows.

### Mental models that belong here
- Founder control panels
- Recurring decision audits
- Operating rhythms
- Offer clarity
- Proposal systems
- Delegation by system
- Constraint-based prioritization

### Visual objects that work best
- Control panels
- Operating maps
- Loops
- Ladders
- Scorecards
- Before/after systems

### Paid assets this can lead to
- Founder control panels
- Proposal factory templates
- Weekly operating rhythm kits
- Offer clarity canvases
- SOP packs

### Exclude from this pillar
- Hustle content
- Founder motivation posts
- Generic productivity hacks
- Tool stacks without an operating model
- Growth advice with no system behind it

## 3. Capital & Narrative

### What this pillar teaches
How to build trust through narrative, evidence, proof, investor communication, fundraise preparation, and data room logic.

### Audience pressure it addresses
Founders and operators need to convert traction, progress, and strategic judgment into a clear story that investors, advisors, partners, or senior stakeholders can trust.

### Mental models that belong here
- Proof stacks
- Narrative spines
- Investor update logic
- Data room readiness
- Evidence hierarchy
- Risk reversal
- Traction-to-repeatability mapping

### Visual objects that work best
- Proof stacks
- Narrative spines
- Evidence banks
- Scorecards
- Teardown maps
- Decision trees

### Paid assets this can lead to
- Investor update templates
- Fundraise proof banks
- Data room planning canvases
- Narrative audit checklists
- Proof-stack trackers

### Exclude from this pillar
- Fundraising hype
- Valuation promises
- Investor name-dropping
- Pitch-deck aesthetics without narrative logic
- Generic startup advice with no proof structure

## 4. Corporate Leverage

### What this pillar teaches
How ambitious corporate professionals globally can communicate upward, build visibility, convert work into proof, influence stakeholders, and make operating value legible.

### Audience pressure it addresses
Corporate operators are often expected to handle ambiguity, show impact, manage stakeholders, and operate above their title without a clear system for proving their value.

### Mental models that belong here
- Evidence banks
- Visibility panels
- Stakeholder maps
- Appraisal proof systems
- Upward communication models
- Meeting-to-decision conversion
- Influence through clarity

### Visual objects that work best
- Evidence banks
- Visibility panels
- Stakeholder maps
- Decision trees
- Scorecards
- Teardown maps
- Before/after systems

### Paid assets this can lead to
- Appraisal evidence trackers
- Stakeholder update templates
- Visibility panel templates
- Meeting notes-to-decision systems
- Communication canvases

### Exclude from this pillar
- Corporate jargon with no operator utility
- Office politics gossip
- Motivation-only career advice
- Resume tips without operating proof
- Tool tutorials disconnected from communication or influence
