# OLS Analytics Reviewer

## Purpose
Review manual Instagram/content performance metrics and convert them into content, format, product, and research learnings.

## When to use
Use weekly or monthly after metrics are manually added to analytics trackers.

## Inputs required
- Updated manual metrics
- Review period
- Optional business goal
- Optional product interest notes

## Source files to read
- `06-analytics/post-performance-tracker.csv`
- `06-analytics/payment-performance-tracker.csv`
- `06-analytics/analytics-review-template.md`
- `04-content-bank/topic-bank.csv`
- `02-editorial-system/thought-leadership-doctrine.md`
- `03-carousel-system/carousel-quality-checklist.md`
- `05-products/product-ladder.md`

## Output files to create or update
If explicitly requested:

- `06-analytics/weekly-review-[date].md`
- `06-analytics/monthly-review-[date].md`
- `04-content-bank/topic-bank.csv`
- `04-content-bank/pillar-wise-ideas.md`

Analytics review should assess:

- top posts by saves
- top posts by shares
- top posts by profile visits
- top posts by bio clicks
- weak posts
- best pillars
- best visual formats
- best archetypes
- best hooks
- product-interest signals
- qualitative replies/comments
- topics to double down on
- topics to reframe
- topics to retire
- product or lead magnet signals

## Step-by-step procedure
1. Read trackers and review template.
2. Identify performance leaders and laggards.
3. Compare performance to content format and topic.
4. Extract learning.
5. Recommend:
   - double down
   - reframe
   - retire
   - research deeper
   - productize
6. Suggest next content/product actions.

## Guardrails
- Do not create dashboards.
- Do not create scripts.
- Do not create charts unless explicitly asked.
- Do not infer too much from tiny data.
- Do not declare product-market fit from weak signals.
- Do not edit trackers unless explicitly asked.

## Done when
- Performance review is complete.
- Learnings are summarized.
- Next actions are clear.

## Summary format
1. Review period
2. Best-performing posts/formats
3. Weak posts and likely reasons
4. Content learnings
5. Product-interest signals
6. Recommended next actions
