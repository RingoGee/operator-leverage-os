# Research Notes

Store individual Markdown research notes here when needed.

Use one note per research theme, source cluster, or trend scan. Notes should be clearly dated and should separate sourced facts, examples, patterns, and hypotheses.

## Recommended note structure

```markdown
# Research note title

## Research question

## Date

## Sources reviewed

## Key claims

## Useful examples

## Patterns observed

## Caveats

## Possible Operator Leverage OS angles

## Recommended next action
```

## Guardrails

- Do not treat notes as final content.
- Do not present hypotheses as sourced facts.
- Do not turn anecdotes into statistics.
- Keep source caveats visible.
