# Research Bank

This folder stores research inputs for Operator Leverage OS.

It is **not** a final content folder. It is a place to store source records, claims, examples, caveats, and mappings from research to future content ideas.

The research bank supports:

- Grounded content ideas
- Credible evidence slides
- Public examples
- Workplace patterns
- Mini-cases
- Research-backed claims
- Trend scanning
- Topic-bank updates
- Carousel brief evidence layers
- Product insight signals

## Research honesty rules

Research must remain honest.

- Examples should not be presented as statistics.
- Unsourced observations should be labeled as field observations or hypotheses.
- Research-backed claims should include source, year/date, caveat, and confidence.
- Do not invent statistics.
- Do not use “studies show” without a specific source.
- Do not convert weak examples into universal claims.
- Do not use outdated sources for current trends unless marked historical.
- Do not treat anecdotes as research.
- Do not create final content directly from research without a brief.

## Recommended workflow

Research scan
→ source-log entry
→ claim-bank entry
→ example-bank entry
→ research-to-content mapping
→ topic-bank candidate
→ carousel brief
→ production draft
→ design prompt
→ visual generation prompt

## Files in this folder

- `source-log.csv` — source-level records and caveats.
- `claim-bank.csv` — extracted claims, statistics, findings, and content angles.
- `example-bank.csv` — public examples, workplace patterns, and mini-case inputs.
- `research-to-content-map.md` — rules for turning research into content safely.
- `research-notes/` — optional Markdown notes for research themes, source clusters, or trend scans.
